// About Zen

What is Zen?

Zen is an independent early-stage project focused on researching practical ways people and technology can work together to address real-world problems.

Its direction includes infrastructure, safety, human-centered technology, and mental-health initiatives.

▥

Infrastructure

Exploring how technology can support infrastructure and help communities respond to challenges.

⬡

Safety

Researching concepts around safety, communication, emergency awareness, and coordination.

◎

Human-Centered Technology

Technology should support people—not blindly replace human judgment.

✳

Mental Health

Supporting mental-health ideas and initiatives, both with and without technology.

// Our Principle

The Human Stays in Control

Zen's human-in-the-loop philosophy centers human judgment, verification, and accountability—especially when a system could affect safety.

Technology should help people make informed decisions, not remove responsible people from the process.
