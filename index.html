Our Philosophy

Human in the Loop

Technology can assist people, but important decisions must not lose sight of human judgment, responsibility, and safety.

People Must Remain Part of the Decision

Zen's human-in-the-loop philosophy emphasizes meaningful human oversight in AI-assisted and automated systems, especially when decisions could affect safety, rights, or well-being.

The goal is not to give technology unlimited authority. It is to explore tools that can support people while making responsibilities, limitations, and opportunities for intervention clear.

  • Human review for important decisions
  • Clear responsibility and accountability
  • Verification before safety-critical actions
  • Respect for people and their well-being
  • Careful evaluation of risks and limitations

Frequently Asked Questions

Get to Know Zen

Have questions about the mission, development, or future direction? Start here.

What is Zen Infrastructure Technologies?

Zen is an early-stage initiative exploring infrastructure technology, safety and security, human-centered systems, and mental health. Its goal is to investigate ideas that could contribute to a better future.

Is Zen already a fully operating company?

Zen is in an early stage of development. This website describes its mission and areas of work; it should not be taken as a claim that every proposed service is operational or commercially available.

Are Zen's projects available to the public?

Availability depends on the individual project. Research topics, concepts, prototypes, and long-term plans are not necessarily finished products or public services.

Why does Zen emphasize human oversight?

Zen's philosophy is that technology should assist people rather than remove human responsibility from important decisions. Human review, clear accountability, and appropriate safeguards are central goals.

How does Zen approach mental health?

Mental health is part of Zen's broader mission. The vision includes exploring supportive ideas both with and without technology. Zen should not be understood as a substitute for qualified mental health care.

How can I follow Zen's progress?

Check this website for updates. The Zen Insider newsletter is a planned way to share news and development updates once a real email subscription service is connected.

Can I collaborate with Zen?

Potential collaboration can be explored as the initiative develops. Any actual opportunity, responsibilities, and terms would need to be discussed and agreed upon separately.
