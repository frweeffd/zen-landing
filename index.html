Questions & Answers

Understanding Zen.

What is Zen Infrastructure Technologies?

Zen is an early-stage initiative exploring infrastructure innovation, responsible technology, cybersecurity, and community well-being.

What is Zen Command Center?

A browser-based fictional infrastructure dashboard with interactive alerts, simulated city nodes, activity logs, and human-review workflows.

What is ZIRA?

ZIRA stands for Zen Intelligence & Risk Analysis. It is a planned division researching technical risks, defensive cybersecurity, and responsible information analysis.

Are these systems connected to real cities?

No. All dashboard scenarios and risk exercises are fictional demonstrations.

Is Zen an AI company?

Zen is not AI-only. It explores infrastructure and human-centered technology with human accountability.
